About ADAA · Doha, Qatar

Care is a craft.
We treat it like one.

ADAA Health Care Trading is a Doha-based partner for medical devices, medicines, nutritional supplements, skincare and derma cosmetics. We connect care providers, retailers and homes to products chosen with precision — and support them long after the order.

01 / Our story

Built to make good care easier to reach.

We work at the point where healthcare and everyday wellbeing meet. One partner for clinical devices, trusted medicines, nutritional support and the skincare people choose for themselves. A range that makes sense together, backed by a team that understands the difference between simply supplying a product and standing behind it.

Good care depends on what stands behind it: the provenance of a product, the rigor of a supplier, the responsiveness of a partner. ADAA was built around that belief. We source with intention, curate with care and stay close long after the order is placed.

“The details are not the details. In care, they are the whole story.”

02 / What drives us

A wider view.
