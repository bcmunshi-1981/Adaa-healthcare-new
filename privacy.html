This note explains what happens to information when you visit this website or get in touch. It is written to be read, not skimmed past.

UpdatedOctober 2026

The short version

This is a simple marketing site. The enquiry form does not send data to a server; it opens your own email app. We only receive what you choose to email us.

Who we are

ADAA Health Care Trading, Office No. 2/B, Ground Floor, Building No. 76, Street No. 362, Area 34, Doha, Qatar. For any privacy question, write to info@adaahealthcare.com.

What this website collects

This website does not include analytics, advertising pixels or account registration. It does not set its own marketing cookies.

The enquiry form is handled in your browser. When you submit it, your email app opens with a pre-filled message. Nothing is transmitted to us until you choose to send that email from your own email account.

What we receive by email

If you email us or use the form to start an email, we receive the details you include — typically your name, email address, phone number, area of interest and message. We use those details to respond to your enquiry and manage any follow-up conversation.

  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We keep enquiries only as long as needed for the conversation or a legitimate business record.
  • You can ask us to correct or delete your enquiry details by emailing the address above.

Third-party services

The contact page includes a Google Maps embed so you can find our office. Loading that map may allow Google to receive your IP address and set cookies according to its own policies. If you prefer to avoid this, use the written address instead of interacting with the map.

Changes

If this website starts using a form service, analytics or other tools that change how information is handled, this note will be updated before those tools go live.
