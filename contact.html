A few useful answers

Before you write.

Do you work with businesses as well as individuals?

Yes. We work with clinics, healthcare providers, pharmacies, retailers and individual customers. Tell us which describes you and we'll route your enquiry appropriately.

Can you source a product that isn't listed?

Our published portfolio is a starting point, not a ceiling. Share the product details, quantity and destination. We will explore the right sourcing route, subject to quality and regulatory considerations.

Do you deliver outside Qatar?

Worldwide delivery can be arranged. Lead times, documentation and terms depend on the product and destination, so we confirm these as part of your enquiry.

How do I request pricing?

Use the enquiry form with the category, quantities and delivery destination. The more specific you are, the faster we can respond with a meaningful answer.
